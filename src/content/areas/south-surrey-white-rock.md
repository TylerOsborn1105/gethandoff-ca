## What the free hour looks like

The first hour is free. I come to your place in South Surrey or White Rock. I ask about your week, you show me a couple of things on your screen, and I teach you one thing you can use the next day. Within a day you get a one-page report: what I'd fix, what I wouldn't bother with, and what each would cost. The report is yours either way. If you don't need me, I'll tell you.

You don't need to prepare a presentation. The useful part is the real inbox, the real spreadsheet, or the folder of quotes. People describe the work as it should be. The screen shows what it is.

## The admin it takes on

These are the everyday jobs, spelled out on the [admin jobs](/answers/admin-jobs-to-hand-off/) page.

- Customer questions you answer every week, drafted in your words for you to check and send.
- Quotes and estimates that currently wait for a quiet hour. Notes in, a draft out, and you still own every number.
- A morning list of who is owed a call, so follow-up does not depend on memory.
- Receipts and rough notes turned into something you can file or send, with a person checking names, dates and totals.

Staff do not get replaced. They stop doing the retyping and get back to the work you hired them for.

## Teaching, setup, and builds

I teach first. I only build when it's worth it.

Teaching is a half-day with you and your team, on your real work, in plain words, for $2,000. Keeping it going is $900 a month: a monthly session, a line for questions between, and a written cheat sheet each month.

Setup means the tools you already pay for, fitted to how you work. ChatGPT, Copilot, the inbox, the spreadsheets. That is $1,500 to $3,500. The point is that the tool does the job without you babysitting it.

A custom build is only for the job nothing off the shelf fits. It starts at $5,000, it runs on your accounts, and you own it. You can take my access away. An optional care plan, for those builds only, starts at $600 a month.
