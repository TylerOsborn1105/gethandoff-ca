Richmond is a short drive through the tunnel from Delta, and it has one of the most varied small business scenes in the Lower Mainland: family restaurants and retail around City Centre, import and distribution businesses near the river and the airport, professional offices, and the shops and services of Steveston.

## What the free hour looks like here

I come to your shop or office, and you walk me through a normal week, especially the parts you'd happily never do again. I'll teach you one thing you can use the next day, and send a one-page report within a day with what I'd fix, what I wouldn't bother with, and what each would cost. No pressure, and if you don't need me, I'll say so.

## The kind of work I see in Richmond

- **Customer messages in more than one language.** AI is genuinely good at drafting and checking replies across languages, as long as a fluent person reviews what goes out.
- **Orders, invoices and supplier emails** in businesses that buy and sell with other businesses, where the same information gets typed into three different places.
- **Busy front counters and phones**, answering the same questions about hours, stock, bookings and pickups.
- **Owners who are also the office manager**, catching up on paperwork after closing.

The pattern is the same from Steveston village to offices along No. 3 Road and warehouses near Bridgeport and the airport. The work itself is fine. The hours go to messages, orders, and the same details typed in more than one place.

## A note on customer information

Many Richmond businesses handle customer details across borders, with suppliers and customers overseas. That's worth getting right before you put customer information into any AI tool.
