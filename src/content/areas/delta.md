## The kind of work I see in Delta

Delta has a mix you don't find everywhere: village shops and restaurants, professional offices, family farms, and a lot of light industry and distribution around Tilbury and Annacis. Very different businesses, but the same paperwork problems:

- **Quotes and orders that get rebuilt from scratch**, especially in shops that sell to other businesses.
- **The same customer questions, answered over and over**, by phone and email.
- **Owners doing the books on Sunday nights**, keying in receipts and chasing invoices.
- **Follow-ups that slip in busy season**, because nobody has time to remember who's owed a call.
