## The busywork it helps with

These are the ordinary admin jobs, the kind already described on the [admin jobs](/answers/admin-jobs-to-hand-off/) page.

- **The same customer replies, over and over.** Your hours, what's included, how long something takes, when someone is coming. A draft can sit there in your words. You read it and send it.
- **Quotes that wait until the evening.** You take notes with the customer, then face a blank page later. A first draft can be ready when you sit down. You still check every number before it goes out.
- **Callbacks nobody is tracking.** A quote went out and was never chased. A customer went quiet. A short list each morning says who to call.
- **Receipts, bills and rough notes.** Photos of receipts that have to be keyed in, or scribbles from a call that have to become an email or a job sheet. The typing is the part that can move.

A person still checks the work before it goes anywhere. The aim is to stop doing the typing, not to stop paying attention. If a job is not worth paying to fix, the report says so.
