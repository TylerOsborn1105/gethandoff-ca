The thread from Friday is still unanswered, and the file they mean is in Drive under a name you do not remember. That is the Monday pile. Gemini in Gmail is the assistant inside that inbox for a small business on Google Workspace.

**The short answer:** summarize the thread, draft the reply, and check it against the email before you send it. Finding a file in Drive, and using Gemini Notebook on a stack of documents, depends on the plan.

## Where do you find Gemini in Gmail?

Gmail's help page "Collaborate with Gemini in Gmail" says the side panel needs an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. On a computer, open Gmail and click Ask Gemini at the top right. Type the request and click Submit.

Help me write is separate. The drafting help page says to click Compose, or open a reply, and use the prompt bar at the bottom of that window. If the bar is missing, click Help me write, then Create. If Ask Gemini is missing, check the plan, and ask whoever manages the account whether Gemini is on for your user.

## How do you summarize a long thread?

Gmail's help page on AI Overview conversation summaries says to open the thread and click Summarize this email. It says this works on a work, school, or personal Google account, and that Smart features in Gmail, Chat, and Meet, plus Smart features in Google Workspace, have to be on. Turning those off also turns off other writing help in Gmail.

Use the summary to remember who asked for what, then scroll the thread. The same page says suggestions can be inaccurate, and that you should not rely on them for medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice. If a task has a due date, Remind me can add it to Google Tasks or Google Calendar. Confirm the date yourself.

## How do you draft a reply that sounds like the business?

Google's drafting page says to name who you are writing to, what they should do, and the tone. For accuracy, point at a specific email or file.

```
Reply to this thread.
Tell them Thursday at 2 still works.
Do not offer a discount.
Keep it under 80 words.
If a fact is not in this thread, write [CHECK THIS] instead of guessing.
```

The page says Help me write can pull a time or a booking code from your other emails and Drive files. Click Sources. If it grabbed the wrong booking, fix the line yourself. Formalize, Friendly, and Shorten are on that bar. You read the draft, then you send it.

## How do you find a file in Drive?

Drive's help page says an eligible plan can take a normal phrase in the search bar. Go to drive.google.com, type a file type, a topic, and a person, then click the citations before you trust the file. A question about what is inside the files can show an AI Overview, also with citations.

The edition comparison leaves the Drive side panel, and Gemini in Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Slides, unchecked for Business Starter. It checks them for Business Standard and Business Plus. On Starter, use the Gmail buttons. Drive search with Gemini is the step up.

## What is Gemini Notebook?

When the pile is quotes, notes, and PDFs, Google's Canadian Workspace page lists "AI research assistant (Gemini Notebook)" on the Standard plan, under "All of Starter, and."

The product page calls it Gemini Notebook, formerly NotebookLM. It answers from sources you upload, including Docs and Slides from Drive, and it shows citations. Uploaded data is not used for model training, and sources stay private unless you share a notebook. The edition comparison lists it on all three business plans, with higher limits on Standard and Plus. The Gmail reply does not need a notebook.

On [Google's Canadian Workspace page](https://workspace.google.com/intl/en_ca/), checked on 16 October 2026, prices are in Canadian dollars. Plans are billed monthly. A one-year commitment saves 16%. The higher figure is month to month. The lower figure is the commitment price.

## What should you check before you send it?

Google's help pages say the suggestion can be wrong. The summary should match the thread. Sources on a Help me write draft should point at the right emails. Times, prices, and promises should be ones you will stand behind. Replace or cut any [CHECK THIS]. The help page says not to rely on Gemini for medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice.

Google's Generative AI Privacy Hub, last updated 29 August 2025, applies when you use Gemini on a qualifying Workspace edition. It says your content is not human reviewed or used for generative AI model training outside your domain without permission. Prompts and responses in Gemini in Workspace are not kept after the session ends. Text you insert into the email follows ordinary Workspace retention. A personal Gmail login is a different setup. ## Questions owners ask

### Where is Gemini in Gmail?

On a computer, Gmail's help page says to click Ask Gemini at the top right. Help me write is the prompt bar in the compose window. Google's edition comparison, checked 16 October 2026, lists both on Business Starter, Business Standard, and Business Plus.

### Can Gemini in Gmail summarize a long thread?

Yes. Open the thread and click Summarize this email, with Smart features turned on. Google says the summary can be wrong, so read the thread before you act on it.

### Which Google Workspace plan includes Gemini in Gmail?

Google's Canadian page, checked 16 October 2026, lists Gemini in Gmail on Starter at CA$11 per user a month, or CA$9.20 with a one-year commitment. Standard and Plus include it too. Prices are Canadian dollars, billed monthly.

### Can I search Drive with Gemini on every business plan?

The edition comparison lists Gemini in Drive on Business Standard and Business Plus, not on Business Starter. Type a plain phrase in the Drive search bar and open the citations.

### Is Workspace content used to train Gemini?

Google's Generative AI Privacy Hub, last updated 29 August 2025 and checked 16 October 2026, says that on a qualifying Workspace edition your content is not used for model training outside your domain without permission. That hub covers Help me write and the side panel. A personal Gmail account is a different setup.

### What is Gemini Notebook?

Google calls it Gemini Notebook, formerly NotebookLM. It answers from your sources, with citations, and the page says uploads are not used for model training. Standard and Plus have higher limits than Business Starter.
