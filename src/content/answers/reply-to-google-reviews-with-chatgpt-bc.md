## Why does the reply wait until closing?

You know what you would say at the counter. Writing it on a public page feels different. A happy review gets the same thank-you every time, because you are busy. An unhappy review sits longer, because you do not want the wrong sentence where everyone can read it.

## How do you reply to a review on Google?

Google's help page "Manage customer reviews" says you must verify your business before you can reply. After the business is verified, you reply on your Business Profile.

1. Go to your Business Profile.
2. Select Read reviews.
3. Next to the review you want to reply to, select Reply.
4. Enter your reply in the box.
5. Select Reply.

Do the drafting before step 4. The box is where the checked words go.

## What happens after you post the reply?

Google reviews the reply against its content policies. If it is not approved, you are asked to edit it. A reply usually takes up to 10 minutes to review. Sometimes that can take up to 30 days.

If it is approved, it is public under the customer's review. It appears as your business. Your personal name is not shown. The customer is notified, and they can still change their review. If they do, the date changes to the latest update.

To change or remove your reply, open Read reviews, then select Edit or Delete under it. If you edit, select Update when you are done.

Google's tips page says replies are public, and that reviews show next to your Business Profile in Maps and Search.

## How do you get ChatGPT to sound like you?

Start with a short note about how you talk. You paste it every time, above the review. Write it the way you would explain it to a new hire.

```
I run a small [shop, clinic, or trade] in [Tsawwassen, Ladner, South Surrey, or White Rock].
I write the way I talk at the counter. Short, friendly, and plain.
I sign replies with [your name].
I do not use slogans.
I do not offer a discount in a reply.
Here is one reply I was happy with. Private details are already removed:
[paste one old reply, or describe it in one sentence]
```

Save that note on your phone. The next review starts with the note, then the prompt below.

## What prompt works for a happy review?

Google's tips page says to skip the same thank-you for everyone. Mention the specific thing they wrote. Keep it short. Google says that if a reply is too long, people may stop reading before the point. It also says to be conversational, and to avoid using the reply to offer deals or promotions.

```
Using the voice note above, write one reply to this Google review.
Three or four short sentences.
Thank them once.
Mention one specific thing they said.
Do not offer a deal or a discount.
Do not add any detail that is not in the review.
If you are unsure, write [CHECK THIS].
Stars: [number]
Review:
[paste the review]
```

Then do this.

1. Read the draft out loud.
2. Change any line you would not say at the counter.
3. Add one sentence only you would write.
4. Take out anything marked [CHECK THIS], or replace it with a fact you know.
5. Paste the finished reply into Google yourself.

## What prompt works for an unhappy review?

Google's tips page, checked on 13 October 2026, says to be polite and keep it short. Keep the reviewer's private information out, and skip any personal attack. If it is complicated, ask them to phone or email so you can talk in private. Acknowledge a real mistake. Do not take responsibility for something outside your control. Use their name and the specific point they raised. A sign-off with your name or initials is something Google suggests. Reply promptly.

```

Keep it to a few short sentences.
Use their first name if the review shows it.

Do not add private details.

If it needs a longer conversation, ask them to call or email [your phone or email].
Sign it with [your name].
```

## What should stay out of the reply?

A few rules on Google's tips page are easy to miss when you are rushing a draft.

Your reviewers are already customers. Google says to avoid using the response to offer deals or promotions. Share a detail that fits their visit instead.

Google also says reviews must reflect a genuine experience. Offering free or discounted goods or services in exchange for a review, a changed review, or removal of a negative review is prohibited. Keep that offer out of the prompt and out of the reply. If a review breaks Google's policies, Google says you can flag it from the help page.

## Questions owners ask

### Can I use ChatGPT to reply to a Google review?

You can use it to draft the words. You still read the draft, take out anything that is not true, and post the reply yourself on your Google Business Profile. Google says you must verify the business before you can reply.

### Are replies to Google reviews public?

Yes. Google says that if a reply is approved, it is posted in public under the customer's review. The person who wrote the review is notified. They can still change their review after reading your reply.

### Will my personal name show on the Google reply?

Google says the reply appears as your business, and your personal name is not shown as the replier. Google's tips for negative reviews also suggest a sign-off with your name or initials inside the words of the reply.

### Can I offer a discount in a Google review reply?

Google says to avoid using the reply to offer deals or promotions. Google also says that offering incentives in exchange for a review, a changed review, or removal of a negative review is prohibited.

### How do I reply to a bad review for a shop in Tsawwassen or Ladner?

Draft a short reply that uses their name, names the specific problem, skips private details, and asks them to phone or email if it needs a longer talk. Read it out loud, then post it yourself. The prompts on this page are written for that.
