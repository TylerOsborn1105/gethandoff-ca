The phone rings while you are with a client in Ladner, or with someone at the counter in Tsawwassen, and you cannot pick up. The voicemail is still there at supper. Sometimes the work has gone to whoever answered. If that is the headache, this is a plain look at an AI receptionist for small business Canada: what it does, what it cannot do, and when it is worth paying for.

**The short answer:** It picks up and takes a message. It can book or text only if you loaded your own prices and times. It is worth a look when missed calls are lost jobs. It is a poor buy when a clearer voicemail and a same-day call back would do it. Read the note before you treat it as fact.

*Privacy notes are from the federal privacy commissioner and the BC commissioner's business guide, checked 20 October 2026. This is not legal advice.*

## What does an AI receptionist actually do?

It greets the caller, asks why they are phoning, takes a name and a number, and sends you a summary. Some can book, text, or pass the call through, if you said which calls should. It only knows the hours, services, and prices you gave it.

Prices vary a lot between products, from free trials to a few hundred dollars a month, and change often. Check the current plan page, in Canadian dollars, before you sign up. Handoff does not sell these.

## What can it not do?

It cannot see the job. A quote that needs a visit still needs you. If a price or a date was not in your notes, have it take a message. A transcript can drop a word, so read it before you promise a day. An angry caller, a safety problem, or a health detail is a person-to-person call. If the tool records, say so in the greeting.

## Is it worth it for your kind of work?

**You are with clients all day, or alone at the counter.** Missed calls are often new customers. Catching the name and number can mean a booked day. Put the fee next to what one real job is worth. I will not invent that number.

**You already answer.** Say when you call back, and do it the same day. That covers a lot of owners in Delta, South Surrey, and White Rock.

**The call needs a visit, or you want the tool so you never talk to people.** It can hold the lead. It should not quote the job, and you still call back. After hours fits only when the work cannot wait until morning.

## What should you try before you pay?

1. **Say when you will call back.** Your name, the business, and a time you will keep.
2. **Write down who is owed a call.** The miss is usually the call that never goes back out.
3. **Text the same day** from your own phone. If writing it is the stuck part, [ChatGPT prompts for small business: 10 you can copy today](/answers/chatgpt-prompts-for-small-business/) has one for a missed call. Leave the name out while you draft.

## Do you have to say the call is recorded?

The federal privacy commissioner's page on recording customer calls, checked 20 October 2026, says a recording collects personal information. If PIPEDA applies, you need a reasonable purpose, you tell the caller, you state that purpose narrowly, and you get consent. Staying on the line usually counts as consent. A voiceprint (using the voice to identify someone later) can need a clear yes. If recording is not essential, offer another way, unless an exception applies. Where PIPEDA does not apply, the page calls this best practice. People can ask to hear the recording. Keep it only as long as the purpose needs.

The BC [guide for businesses](https://www.oipc.bc.ca/documents/guidance-documents/1371), October 2015 (5th publication), checked the same day, says PIPA covers information about an identifiable person. PIPEDA applies in BC to federally regulated work (banks, telephone companies, airlines, shipping, railways) and may apply when information about people from other provinces is affected. Section 10(1) says you state the purposes before you collect, and name someone who can answer questions. You can say that on the call.

Say you are recording so you can call back about the job. Do not record "just in case." Health details, card numbers, and anything about a child do not belong in a transcript you have not thought through.
